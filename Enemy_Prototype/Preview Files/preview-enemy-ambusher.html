While the player is busy fighting normal enemies, an ambusher erupts from hiding with a guaranteed surprise hit. Completely invisible until triggered by proximity. The first hit always lands because the player was focused on the existing fight. After revealing, it's weak — but the damage is done. Punishes tunnel vision.
