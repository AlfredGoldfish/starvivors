Card Draft

Pattern
Live Preview
A two-part system. During combat, draw cards into a hand and spend energy to play them — Strike deals damage, Defend adds block, spells do special effects. After combat, draft a new card into your growing deck. Your deck IS your build. Deck management is the strategy.
Combat
Draw 5 → spend energy → play cards → enemy turn → repeat
Draft
After fight, pick 1 of 3 cards to add to deck
Best for
Deck builders, roguelites, tactical card combat
Feel
Synergy hunting, hand management, deck optimization
RPGStrategyRoguelite e.g. Slay the Spire, Monster Train, Inscryption, Balatro
