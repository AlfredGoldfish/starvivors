The playable area shrinks over time. Standing outside the safe zone deals damage. The zone gets smaller at set intervals, forcing players into tighter and tighter spaces. Creates escalating tension as the arena compresses. Warning indicators show when the next shrink is coming.
Trigger
Timer-based (every 30-60s) or continuous
Best for
Battle royale, survival arenas, last-man-standing
Strength
Forces engagement, prevents camping, natural endgame
Feel
Mounting pressure, claustrophobia, urgent decision-making
