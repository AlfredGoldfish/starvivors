Wave → Rest → Wave

Pattern
Live Preview
The core rhythm of wave-based games. Fight a wave of enemies → wave ends → rest period where you shop, upgrade, or heal → next wave begins with harder enemies. This creates natural pacing with tension and release. The rest period is where the player makes meaningful decisions.
Rhythm
FIGHT → CLEAR → REST (shop/upgrade/heal) → FIGHT
Best for
Arena survival, tower defense, wave shooters
Strength
Natural pacing, clear goals, decision moments between waves
Feel
Tension during waves, relief during rest, anticipation before next
ArenaStrategySurvival e.g. Call of Duty Zombies, Kingdom Rush, 20 Minutes Till Dawn
