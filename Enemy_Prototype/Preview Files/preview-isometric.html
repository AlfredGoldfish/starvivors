An angled overhead view that gives the illusion of 3D depth on a 2D canvas. The world is made of diamond-shaped tiles. Objects further "back" appear higher on screen. Creates a rich tactical or RPG feel without true 3D rendering.
Camera
Fixed angle (~30°), diamond tile grid
Best for
Tactics, RPGs, city builders, strategy, management
Strength
Looks richer than flat top-down, natural depth perception
Weakness
Movement can feel unintuitive, harder to implement precisely
RPGStrategySimTactics e.g. Diablo, Into the Breach, SimCity, Hades (partial)
