The game world is divided into discrete rooms. Enter a room, doors lock behind you. Clear all enemies to unlock exits. Walk through a door to transition to the next room. Each room is its own contained encounter.
Camera
Static per room, transitions on door entry
Best for
Dungeon crawlers, roguelikes, Zelda-style exploration
Strength
Each room is a designed encounter, pacing is controlled
Weakness
Transitions interrupt flow, rooms can feel repetitive
