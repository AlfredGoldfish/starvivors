Enter a room — doors slam shut. Kill every enemy to unlock the exits. Choose which door to take: each leads to a different encounter. The rhythm is fight → clear → choose → repeat. Rooms can contain combat, treasure, shops, or traps. The player's route through the dungeon emerges from door choices.
Loop
Enter → doors lock → kill all → doors open → pick exit
Best for
Dungeon crawlers, roguelikes, arena combat, Zelda-likes
Strength
Every room is a self-contained encounter with clear stakes
Feel
Tension → relief → anticipation → tension. Addictive micro-loop
