Thief (carrying gold)
Doesn't deal damage — steals your gold instead. While you're busy fighting other enemies, the thief darts in, snatches currency on contact, then sprints toward the edge to escape. Kill it before it escapes and you get your gold back plus bonus. The dilemma: keep fighting, or chase the thief?
Approach

Steal

Weakness
Low HP, dies in 1-2 hits, drops gold on death
Danger
Economic damage, forces priority decision mid-combat
