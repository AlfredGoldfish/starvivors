Weapon Evolution

Pattern
Live Preview
Weapons automatically evolve when conditions are met — usually kill counts. At each threshold, the weapon visually transforms: shoots faster, does more damage, adds new behaviors (piercing, spread, homing). Watch your basic pea-shooter become a screen-clearing death machine. Evolution milestones create satisfying power spikes.
Trigger
Kill count, time held, or combo with another item
Best for
Arena survivors, shooters, idle games
Strength
Visible power growth, exciting transformation moments
Feel
Anticipation → satisfaction → "let's see the next one"
ArenaShooterIdle e.g. Vampire Survivors, Archero, weapon prestige systems
