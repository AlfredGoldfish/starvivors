On death, this enemy splits into 2-3 smaller copies that continue fighting. Each generation is smaller, faster, and weaker. What looks like one enemy becomes a swarm. Forces the player to manage crowd control — killing carelessly floods the arena. The quintessential "be careful what you shoot" enemy.
Movement
Slow chase (large), faster chase (smaller)
On death
Splits into 2-3 smaller copies per generation
Weakness
Smallest generation doesn't split, dies in 1 hit
Danger
