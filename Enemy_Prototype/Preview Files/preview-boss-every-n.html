Boss Every N Waves

Pattern
Live Preview
Normal waves alternate with boss encounters at set intervals (every 3, 5, or 10 waves). Boss waves feature a single large enemy with a top-screen HP bar, a name, multiple attack patterns, and phase transitions at HP thresholds. Defeating the boss yields big rewards and a full heal. Bosses are milestone moments that punctuate the run.
Rhythm
Wave 1-4: normal → Wave 5: BOSS → repeat harder
Best for
Arena shooters, RPGs, action games with progression
Strength
Epic milestone moments, tests player skill/build
Feel
Anticipation building → intense fight → triumph and reward
ArenaRPGAction e.g. Hades, Gungeon, Risk of Rain, every Zelda
