Variable Gravity

Gravity
Live Preview
Gravity changes strength over time — cycling between heavy, standard, low, and zero-G. The player must adapt their platforming on the fly. A jump that works in heavy gravity overshoots in low gravity. Creates a constantly shifting challenge where timing and muscle memory get disrupted every few seconds.
Cycle
Heavy → Standard → Low → Zero → reverse
Warning
Visible meter + color shift shows current gravity
Challenge
Platforming difficulty shifts mid-jump
