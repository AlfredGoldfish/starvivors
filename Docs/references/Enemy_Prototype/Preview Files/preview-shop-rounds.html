Shop Between Rounds

Pattern
Live Preview
After each wave, a shop screen opens with more items than fit on screen. Browse by scrolling through categories — weapons, consumables, stat upgrades, equipment. Hover over items to see details and compare. Buy what fits your build, skip what doesn't. Reroll for new stock. When ready, continue to the next wave.
Timing
Opens between waves, player-paced (no rush)
Best for
Wave shooters, tower defense, arena survival
Strength
Strategic depth, build planning, meaningful gold spending
Feel
Shopping spree, build optimization, "one more upgrade" pull
ArenaStrategySurvival e.g. CoD Zombies, Brotato, Kingdom Rush, Shop Titans
