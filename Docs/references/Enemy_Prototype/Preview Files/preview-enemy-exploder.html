Two variants of the same threat. Square exploders detonate instantly when they reach the player — no warning, just run. Circle exploders have a visible fuse: they stop, flash, swell up over ~2 seconds, then detonate. The fuse type gives you a window to escape. Both create large AOE blasts. Never stand near either.
