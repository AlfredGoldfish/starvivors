Timed Rounds

Pattern
Live Preview
Each round has a fixed time limit — score as much as possible before time runs out. A big countdown dominates the HUD. When time expires, a results screen shows kills, score, and bonuses. Then the next round begins with harder enemies and a fresh timer. The pressure of the clock changes every decision.
Duration
30s, 60s, 90s, or 120s per round
Best for
Arcade survival, score attack, speedrun challenges
Strength
Urgency, clear structure, easy to compare attempts
Feel
Clock anxiety, rush decisions, "one more round" addiction
ArenaArcadeRunner e.g. Super Hexagon, Minit, WarioWare, arcade time attack
