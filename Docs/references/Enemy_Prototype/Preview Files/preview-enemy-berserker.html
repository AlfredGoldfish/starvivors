Starts slow and manageable — a regular melee enemy. But as its HP drops, it gets faster, bigger, and more aggressive. Below 50% HP it enters a visible rage state: glowing red, shaking, trailing particles. Below 25% it's a screaming missile. The tension: do you burst it down fast, or leave it for last and risk the enrage?
