A deadly combo. The Freezer stays at range and fires icicle projectiles that slow the player on hit. While slowed, the Poisoner — a melee chaser — catches up and applies damage-over-time on contact. Neither is lethal alone. Together: the freeze enables the poison. Classic support + DPS pairing.
