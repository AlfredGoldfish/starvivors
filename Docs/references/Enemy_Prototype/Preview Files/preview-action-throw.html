The player picks up and hurls objects in an arc. Two modes cycle: a lobbed bomb that arcs to a target point and explodes on impact, and a boomerang that travels out and returns to the player, hitting enemies in both directions. Physics-based trajectories with a shadow on the ground showing the landing point.
