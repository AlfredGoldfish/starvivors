Unlike a spawner, a summoner is a full combat enemy — it fights you directly with ranged attacks while maintaining distance. Periodically it stops, channels a summon ritual, and calls in 2-3 reinforcements from off-screen. You're fighting the summoner AND managing its adds simultaneously. Kill it first or get overwhelmed.
Movement
Keeps distance, strafes like a shooter
Attack
Ranged projectiles + periodic summon of adds
Weakness
Vulnerable during summon channel, adds stop spawning on death
Danger
Dual threat: direct damage + growing add count
