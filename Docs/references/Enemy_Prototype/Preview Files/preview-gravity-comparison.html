Gravity: Standard / Low / Heavy

Gravity
Live Preview
Three gravity settings that fundamentally change how a game feels. Heavy gravity means fast falls and low jumps - tight, grounded, precise. Standard is the baseline most players expect. Low gravity means floaty, high jumps and slow falls - spacey, aerial, forgiving. All three jump at the same time so you can see the difference.
Heavy
Fast fall, low jump, tight and grounded
Standard
Balanced fall/jump, expected baseline
Low
Slow fall, high jump, floaty and aerial
PlatformerActionRunner e.g. Celeste (tight), Mario (standard), Lunar Lander (low)
